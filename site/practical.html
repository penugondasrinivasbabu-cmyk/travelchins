01 / Pick your season

Just your sort of day.

All three routes run April–October in our sample 2027 season. We like May for the coast and late September for the high lanes.

04 / A few good questions

The not-so-
small print.

How much walking are we talking?

The Salt Path is 24 km over two walking days, with 520 m of total ascent. The High Lanes is 32 km over three walking days, with 980 m of ascent. The train weekend has two walks totalling 16 km and 240 m of ascent. Coast paths have exposed edges and uneven ground; the hill route has stiles and steep sections. None of these sample routes is step-free.

Can I come on my own?

Yes. These are self-guided trips for one to six people, not scheduled groups. The listed prices assume two people sharing. A single room adds £65 on The Salt Path, £95 on The High Lanes, or £75 on the train weekend, per person for the whole trip.

What is in the price?

Accommodation, breakfasts, offline route notes, daytime desk support, and local arrival/departure travel. Bags are moved on the two point-to-point walking routes; the rail trip includes a three-day Morrow Line rover instead. Prices are in GBP and include all applicable sample taxes. Mainline travel, other meals, drinks, and insurance are extra. Each trip’s day-by-day section has the exact list.

What if the weather turns?

Carry a waterproof, download the notes before leaving the inn, and take the marked inland alternative if the exposed coast or high ground is unsafe. Don’t use a beach shortcut or a tide crossing. For a real trip, check a current local forecast and follow local closures; this fictional site is not navigation or safety advice.

Can I make a booking here?

No. This is an imagined travel brand and a working static-site demo, not a live travel service. The region, timetable, team, routes, prices, and contact address are fictional. There is no payment form or booking backend.

Does this site use cookies or track me?

No analytics, tracking scripts, third-party fonts, or cookies are included in the site code. If JavaScript is on and storage is available, your packing ticks are saved locally in this browser only. “Clear ticks” resets the checklist. Your hosting provider may keep its own access logs.
